Enterprise Legal Intelligence — Contract Risk & Compliance Copilot

Product mind map. Central promise: an explainable AI copilot that reads legal documents of any size or format, extracts obligations, catches contradictions, assesses risk, validates compliance, and suggests fixes — where every finding is traceable to an exact source span and the system abstains rather than guesses.
